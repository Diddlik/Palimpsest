Palimpsest

Wrong-layout typing, quietly fixed.

Sensitivity

How sure Palimpsest must be before it corrects. Lower is more cautious (fewer false positives); higher is more eager.

Cautious Eager 0.50

Layout pairs

Pairs are considered in both directions. Palimpsest reinterprets a word across these layouts and switches to the better one.

Ignore in these apps

Palimpsest stays completely silent in these — terminals, IDEs, games. Pick one from your running apps, or type executable names (one per line, e.g. cmd.exe).

Advanced

—
Click “Set hotkey”, then press a combination (e.g. Ctrl+Shift+K) or a single non-typing key (Pause, F8, Insert). Forces a fix on the current word. Esc cancels.
